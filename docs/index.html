Measurement-burst study · data pipeline

Digital Days: one timeline for four data streams

How ~400 young adults (18–25) move through four 14-day bursts: EMA prompts, AWARE-Light phone sensing, Fitbit through the Google Health API, and donated social-media and AI-chat data, aligned to the same study day. Every link the pipeline depends on is listed below with its status.

Two routes: the AIO, or the direct portal
